About Analytics Snapshots

Analytics snapshots capture the state of your tasks and projects at regular intervals, allowing you to track trends and performance over time. These snapshots include metrics such as task completion rates, pending tasks, overdue tasks, and active user counts.

System-wide snapshots provide a bird's-eye view of your entire organization's performance, while project-specific snapshots focus on individual projects. This historical data can help identify patterns, bottlenecks, and areas for improvement in your workflow.
